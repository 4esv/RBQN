## Decided
- Goal is GPU-first; CBQN is a yardstick only. GPU is exact for integers only
  (no f64 on Metal); float ops stay CPU unless a lossy f32 mode is chosen later.
- Order: sync/upload fix → counters → lazy device values at the registry →
  i64 kernels + exactness guard → fusion → thresholds → sort rewrite →
  shared encoder → subgroups. CPU floor items second.
- Sort kernel rewrite after residency (step 6), not before: it is
  independent, and residency decides how its buffers are owned.
